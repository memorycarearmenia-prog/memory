   but headcount/structure differ.

   canonical = Transit **Connect $9–11k**, David's personal asset leased to the
   company (rent, not capex).

## QR memory-page product (add-on, decided direction)

Digital memorial page reachable via QR code on the headstone. Content:
photo/video gallery + biography + messages from invited family only (no open
public posting — access via link/code, avoids moderation burden). Monetization:
hybrid — bundled free with "Максимум" tariff, paid upsell for other tariffs,
also sellable standalone (no cleaning subscription required). Pricing model:
one-time setup fee + small annual hosting rent (not pure lifetime-hosting,
per market norm) — exact AMD figures not yet finalized in the model.
