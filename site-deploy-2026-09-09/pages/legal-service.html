1. Service area

Cemeteries of Yerevan and the central cemetery of Abovyan (Kotayk). Other regions of Armenia on request, with travel quoted separately. No physical goods are shipped; the service is delivered at the plot and the report is delivered electronically in your account. We do not deliver services outside the Republic of Armenia.

3. Scheduling

After payment we contact you within one business day to agree the visit date(s). Annual-plan visits are spread over the year (roughly quarterly for Optimal, every two months for Maximum) and can be moved to precede memorial dates. We confirm each visit the day before.

4. The report

Published in your account within 24 hours of the visit: photos before and after, a video, GPS coordinates, date and time, and the caretaker’s notes. Reports are stored for the life of your account.

5. Not included

Repairs of stone, re-engraving, restoration of gilding, replacement of tiles or fences, landscaping, and removal of large trees. Minor repairs may be offered as a separate quote after an inspection; we never perform them without your written approval.

6. Access and restrictions

You confirm you are entitled to order care for the plot. Where cemetery administration requires permission for specific works, we will inform you before proceeding.

7. Special offers and trial periods

We currently offer no free or discounted trial periods. Should any promotional offer be introduced, its conditions, duration and the date of the first charge will be stated on the offer itself.
