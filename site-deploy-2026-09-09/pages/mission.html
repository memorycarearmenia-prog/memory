Our mission

To make caring for a family’s resting place possible from anywhere in the world — regularly, respectfully, and with proof.

We don’t sell cleaning. We sell peace of mind.

The people who need us are not looking for a cheaper bucket and rag. They want to stop wondering. Wondering whether the stone is covered in lichen, whether the leaves were ever cleared, whether the flowers placed last spring are still there. Distance and busy lives turn that wondering into quiet guilt.

Our job is to replace wondering with knowing. That is why every visit ends with a report, why the report carries GPS coordinates and a timestamp, and why we will never mark a visit as done without one.

How we work

  • Regularity. Care on a schedule, agreed with you, including memorial dates — not a one-off favour.
  • Gentle methods. Kärcher low-pressure equipment, natural brushes and pH-neutral stone chemistry; nothing abrasive, nothing that stains.
  • Our own team. Local caretakers in olive uniforms, trained by us — not anonymous subcontractors.
  • Transparency. Flat prices, clear terms, and a report you can open at 2 a.m. in Los Angeles.
