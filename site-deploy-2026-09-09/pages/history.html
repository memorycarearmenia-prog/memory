Our history

MemoryCare began with a question most Armenian families know: who is looking after the grave?

In Armenia, tending a family grave has always been the family’s own work — done by whoever lives closest and has the time. Over the last decades that “whoever” has moved: to Los Angeles, Moscow, Lyon, Berlin. The plot stayed where it was.

The founders of MemoryCare, both from Yerevan, kept hearing the same conversation at family tables abroad: “Someone should go and check.” Occasionally a neighbour was asked, or a stranger was paid — and nobody ever really knew what was done. There was no one to call, no schedule, and no proof.

So we built the service we wished existed: a small local team in Yerevan, professional equipment, a fixed price list, and — above all — a report after every single visit, with photos, video, GPS and date. Care you can see, from anywhere.

2025

The idea

Conversations with families in the diaspora and in Yerevan confirm the same gap: no organised, verifiable grave-care service exists in Armenia.

Spring 2026

Company founded

Memory Care LLC is registered in Yerevan. We choose stone-safe methods — Kärcher low-pressure equipment and pH-neutral chemistry — and design the visit report.

Summer 2026

Listening first

A discovery survey with 17 families in Armenia, Europe, the US and Russia shapes the plans: four flat prices, four to six visits a year for most, and a report every time.

Autumn 2026

First season in Yerevan

The team starts in Yerevan cemeteries and Abovyan. Online card payment via Ameriabank; every report in the client’s account.
