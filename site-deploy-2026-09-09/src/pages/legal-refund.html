Refund & cancellation policy

Plain rules: cancel any time, unused visits are refunded pro rata.

Draft for legal review · Effective date: 12 September 2026 (pending final legal sign-off) · {{legalName}}

1. One-time visits (Inspection, Express)

  • Cancelled more than 48 hours before the agreed visit date — full refund.
  • Cancelled less than 48 hours before — refund minus a 10 000 ֏ travel and scheduling fee.
  • After the visit has been performed and the report published — non-refundable.

2. Annual plans (Optimal, Maximum)

  • Cancellation is possible at any time from your account or by writing to {{email}}.
  • The refund equals the plan price minus the visits already performed, valued at the Express single-visit price (60 000 ֏ each).
  • Example: Optimal (160 000 ֏, 4 visits), cancelled after 1 visit → refund 160 000 − 60 000 = 100 000 ֏.
  • If all visits of the plan have been performed, nothing is refundable.

3. Visits we could not perform

If we cannot perform a paid visit (cemetery closed, access denied, force majeure) and cannot reschedule within 30 days, that visit is refunded in full.

4. How refunds are made

Refunds are issued only to the bank card used for the payment, through Ameriabank, within 10 business days of your request. We cannot refund in cash or to another card or account.

5. How to cancel

Write to {{email}} from the e-mail registered in your account, or call {{phone}}, stating the plan and the plot. We confirm the cancellation and the refund amount in writing within one business day.

6. Complaints

If a report does not match the work you expected, tell us within 7 days of its publication. We will review the photos and video with you and, where the work was incomplete, repeat the visit at no cost or refund it.
