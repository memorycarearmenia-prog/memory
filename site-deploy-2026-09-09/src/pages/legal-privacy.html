Privacy & cookie policy

What we collect, why, and how we keep it safe.

Draft for legal review · Effective date: 12 September 2026 (pending final legal sign-off) · Controller: {{legalName}}, {{address}}

1. Who we are

{{legalName}} (“MemoryCare”, “we”) provides care and cleaning of family memorial plots in the Republic of Armenia with photo and video reporting. We are the controller of personal data collected through this website (mc.makyan.com) and the client account.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: name, phone number, e-mail address, password (stored hashed).
  • Order data: cemetery, plot location, the name on the monument, preferred dates, your notes.
  • Payment data: we do not receive or store your card number. Payments are processed by Ameriabank CJSC on its secure page; we receive only the payment status, amount and a transaction reference.
  • Report data: photos, video and GPS coordinates of the plot taken during visits, stored in your account.
  • Contact form: name, e-mail, phone and message.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and cookies (see section 7).

3. Why we use it

To create and manage your account; to schedule and perform visits; to send you reports and service notifications; to process payments and refunds; to answer your requests; to comply with Armenian law (including accounting and anti-fraud obligations of our bank); and to improve the site.

4. Legal basis and retention

We process data to perform the contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, and with your consent where required. Transaction records are kept for at least 3 years from the transaction date as required by our acquiring bank; account data is kept while the account is active and for 3 years after the last order, unless a longer period is required by law.

5. Who we share it with

Ameriabank CJSC (payment processing); our field caretakers (only plot location and visit details); hosting and e-mail providers acting on our instructions. We never sell personal data. Photographs in reports show only the plot you ordered care for.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time by writing to {{email}}. You may also update name, phone, e-mail and password in your account.

7. Cookies

The site uses a session cookie (sid) necessary for signing in, a short-lived cookie remembering the plan you chose before signing in, and Google Maps embeds which may set their own cookies. We do not use advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser; signing in will then not work.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Card data is handled exclusively on the bank’s PCI DSS-certified pages. Access to reports requires your password.
