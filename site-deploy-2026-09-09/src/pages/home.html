Ask a question

How do I know the visit really happened?

Every visit produces a report in your account: photos before and after, a short video, the GPS coordinates of the plot and the exact date and time. Nothing is invoiced as “done” without a report.

Do you use pressure washers on old stone? Is that safe?

We use Kärcher equipment in low-pressure mode together with professional pH-neutral chemistry designed for natural stone. Granite, marble and tuff are cleaned without abrasion; fragile or damaged surfaces are cleaned by hand with soft brushes.

What if the plot is larger than 16 m² or has more than 3 monuments?

Choose the Custom option and describe the plot — we will quote a personal price within one business day. Everything else stays the same: the same equipment, the same reports.

I live in Yerevan — is this service still for me?

Yes. Many local clients simply do not have time for regular visits. We handle the schedule for you, including a one-time visit before a memorial date.

How and when do I pay?

By bank card (Visa, Mastercard, ArCa) in Armenian drams through Ameriabank’s secure payment page. One-time visits are paid before the visit; annual plans are paid once for the whole year and delivered over 12 months.

Can I cancel an annual plan?

Yes. You can cancel at any time from your account or by writing to us; unused visits are refunded pro rata according to our refund & cancellation policy.

Do you need documents or permission to access the plot?

For cleaning and care of an existing plot no special permission is required in Yerevan cemeteries. We only ask you to confirm that you are a relative or authorised by the family, and to give us the plot location.

What happens if you find damage?

We photograph it, describe it in the report and suggest what can be done. Minor repairs can be quoted separately; we never carry out work you have not approved.
