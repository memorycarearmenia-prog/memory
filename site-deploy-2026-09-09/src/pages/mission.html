Our mission

To make caring for a family’s resting place possible from anywhere in the world — regularly, respectfully, and with proof.

A plot that is cared for, and known to be cared for

Most people who come to us are not looking for a cheaper way to clean a stone. They want to stop wondering whether it has been done at all — whether the lichen was ever removed, whether last spring's flowers are long gone, whether anyone has been there since. Distance and busy lives turn that not-knowing into a quiet, ongoing worry.

Our job is to replace that worry with certainty. That is why every visit ends with a report, why the report carries GPS coordinates and a timestamp, and why we will never mark a visit as done without one.

How we work

  • Regularity. Care on a schedule, agreed with you, including memorial dates — not a one-off favour.
  • Gentle methods. Kärcher low-pressure equipment, natural brushes and pH-neutral stone chemistry; nothing abrasive, nothing that stains.
  • Our own team. Local caretakers in olive uniforms, trained by us — not anonymous subcontractors.
  • Transparency. Flat prices, clear terms, and a report you can open at 2 a.m. in Los Angeles.
