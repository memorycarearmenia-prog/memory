Our values

Five things we hold to at every plot, on every visit.

Respect

A plot is a place someone loved. We work quietly, carefully and without haste, and we never photograph other families’ graves.

Evidence

Every visit is documented: before and after, video, GPS, date. If it isn’t in the report, it didn’t happen.

Regularity

Care is a rhythm, not an event. Four or six visits a year keep a plot the way it was meant to look.

Honesty

Flat prices, no hidden surcharges, and a refund policy in plain language. If we find damage, we tell you — we don’t sell you repairs you didn’t ask for.

Craft

Professional equipment and stone-safe chemistry, used the way restorers use them. Granite, marble and tuff each get their own approach.
